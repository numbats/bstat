ETX5900 S2 2026 quiz bank: adaptation for the ETX1100 eight-week intensive

The source bank has 11 original weeks, usually 20 questions each. S3 Q7 below means Question 7 in source Week 3, not intensive Week 3. It contains marked answers and should remain an instructor source file. Keep means the statistical task is suitable; all retained questions still need answers and solution cues removed before student release. Adapt means retain the skill but replace data, context, or wording. Exclude means omit from the core eight-week quizzes.

The original Elecmart, Hytex, PlanFinan, Health care, Hospital Quality, and Data for quiz 11 workbooks were not found in this project. The embedded tables and image references in the Markdown file do supply some questions without those workbooks. Current ETX1100 workbooks can supply replacement data, but the old numerical answers must be recalculated.

Material outside the core sequence

  • S6 Q1–20: The old Week 6 is a chi-square test block. Exclude its chi-square hypotheses, expected-count calculations, test statistics, critical values, and p-values from the core quizzes unless the chief examiner confirms chi-square remains examinable. The printed contingency tables in S6 Q3–12 could instead support new Week 2 or Week 5 questions on conditional percentages and independence as a concept (LO2/LO4); these would be new questions, not unchanged copies.
  • S11 Q1–20: The old Week 11 is seasonal indices, trend forecasts, MAD/MSFE, and spreadsheet forecasting. Exclude from core quizzes because full time-series forecasting is not in the intensive content spine or named 2026 outcomes. A new Week 1 chart-choice question could use a time-series plot, but the original questions mostly ask forecasting methods. Its final Q9 appears after Q20 and should be moved if this bank is ever republished.

Before any questions go to students

  1. Remove answer leakage. This bank has [x] selections, bold numerical answers, and worked conclusions in stems. Draft clean student versions and keep a separate answer key.
  2. Replace missing-data items deliberately. No old named quiz workbooks were found here. Current weekly files are useful replacements, but their variables and answers are not interchangeable with Elecmart or PlanFinan. Recompute every key from the final released dataset.
  3. Repair known defects. S2 Q3 has no defensible choice; S4 Q13 has an incomplete answer and S4 Q11–14 have ambiguous distribution notation; S4 Q16 lacks its number; S7 Q19 states a test statistic of 0.077 although (795−775)/(75/√30) ≈ 1.461; S8 Q13 uses an impossible proportion; S9 Q19 has an incoherent outcome.
  4. Use questions to assess interpretation. Prefer one calculation plus a denominator, assumption, or business conclusion to several near-identical number-entry items. Keep a balanced mix of conceptual choice, Excel use, and contextual reasoning in each weekly quiz.
  5. Audit figures. Many output screenshots are embedded as base64 images in the source Markdown. Extract and relabel only the figures selected for reuse; replace tiny formula images with editable equations and provide accessible text or tables.
